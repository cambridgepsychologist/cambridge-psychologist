            <h1>Who is suitable?</h1>


            <p>Psychological therapy can be helpful for a wide range of emotional and mental health challenges, including:</p>

            <ul>
                <li>Depression</li>
                <li>Anxiety and panic attacks</li>
                <li>Low self-esteem</li>
                <li>Perfectionism and self-criticism</li>
                <li>Feelings of shame</li>
                <li>Difficulties rooted in childhood experiences</li>
                <li>Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)</li>
                <li>Trauma, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and complex PTSD</li>
                <li>Parenting challenges</li>
                <li>Work-life balance concerns</li>
                <li>University or workplace-related stress</li>
            </ul>

            <p>This list isn’t exhaustive. If you're unsure whether therapy might be helpful for you, please feel free
            to get in touch to schedule a phone conversation.</p>
